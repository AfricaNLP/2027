Call for papers

AfricaNLP 2027 will be held as a one-day hybrid workshop at EACL 2027 in Athens, Greece. The theme is from research to real-world AfricaNLP applications.

Topics

We invite submissions in the following areas:

  • NLP research for languages spoken in Africa, including systems, resources and evaluation methodologies
  • Position papers regarding NLP for African languages
  • Best practices for collecting data and engaging with speaker communities for African languages
  • Other research that advances NLP to the benefit of the African continent and globally

Given this year's theme, we particularly welcome work on moving research artifacts into real use: evaluation with real users, deployment under bandwidth and compute constraints, data governance and consent, and the long-term maintenance of language resources.

Tracks

We accept papers submitted through ARR, the ACL Rolling Review, and by direct submission to the workshop.

Accepted papers may appear in the archival workshop proceedings, or be presented non-archivally so that submitting to AfricaNLP does not prevent publication elsewhere. Long and short papers are welcome in either track. Findings papers are welcome as part of the non-archival programme, and subject to space, Findings authors will be able to present relevant papers as posters.

Programme

One day, with in-person and virtual poster sessions and oral spotlight talks. We expect a significant number of authors to join remotely because of visa restrictions and the cost of travel, and the virtual sessions are a full part of the programme. See attending.

Desk rejections

We work hard to avoid them. Submissions are checked for compliance immediately after the deadline, and authors are given three days to correct any non-compliant submission.
