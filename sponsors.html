Sponsors

AfricaNLP has previously been supported by Google DeepMind, Meta, Lelapa AI, Apple, EAMT, GIZ, Masakhane and DAIR.

Sponsorship pays for speaker travel, participant registration fees for attendees who need them, and internet bandwidth grants for remote participants.

AfricaNLP 2027 is confirmed at EACL 2027 in Athens, and we are seeking sponsors for this edition. If your organisation would like to support the workshop, write to africanlp-2027@googlegroups.com.
